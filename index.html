How to interpret a match

The strongest matches assign each preview Pokémon to a different spawn cluster. If all three Pokémon are listed but two share a cluster, the finder displays a possible match with that limitation explained. Regional forms such as Alolan Raichu are searchable separately. Rarity percentages describe odds within a cluster, not the overall chance of encountering a zone.

Zones with unusual source percentages are searchable with warnings. Seven Special zones have incomplete cluster assignments and are shown only as tentative candidates. Mixed-type zone assembly and zone size aren't verified filters in this dataset.
